Real-time Pokémon battles with real booster packs, in your browser. No terminal, no install: download, unzip,
double-click.

**Start:** you get **3 free booster packs**. Open them (real sets, real pull rates), pick your team from the cards you
pulled, and fight the bots. Wins earn tokens toward more packs: 1 for a 1v1, 2 for a team battle, +1 on hard, +2 on
expert; **10 tokens make a pack**.

## Download

- **Windows 10/11 (64-bit):** `pokeshell-arena-*-windows-x64.zip`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `pokeshell-arena-*-macos-universal.zip`

## First launch (the apps aren't signed)

**Windows**
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (don't run the exe from inside the zip).
2. Double-click `pokeshell-arena.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**,
   then **Run anyway**. It only asks once.

**macOS**
1. Unzip and drag **pokeshell arena** into Applications.
2. Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   On macOS 15 Sequoia or later: double-click it once, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, find
   "pokeshell arena was blocked" and click **Open Anyway**. It only asks once.

The game opens in your browser. Opening the app again while it runs just brings the game back; it closes by itself a
couple of minutes after you close the tab. Your cards are saved in your user folder
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\pokeshell-arena-standalone` / `~/Library/Application Support/pokeshell-arena`).

Check a download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` if you like.
